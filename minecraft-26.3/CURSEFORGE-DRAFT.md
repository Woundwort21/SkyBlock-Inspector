## Description

SkyBlock Inspector is a small client-side crosshair overlay designed for Hypixel SkyBlock. It displays the names of blocks and NPCs, shows crop growth when vanilla block-state data allows it, and offers contextual information for mining materials in known areas.

The HUD is draggable and resizable to 25%, with an in-game settings screen for display toggles, opacity, mining mode and keybind access. It does not require NEU, SkyHanni or another SkyBlock mod.

**Features:**
- Compact tooltip with vanilla icons and configurable HUD position, size and transparency
- Garden crop names and growth bars where growth stages are exposed
- Hypixel NPC nameplate recognition (including Sam and Duke)
- Automatic Dwarven Mines location detection, Mithril and Titanium candidate labels and Breaking Power
- F10 manual fallback and integrated settings/keybinding access

**Important limitations:** Gemstone and Crystal Hollows support is experimental. SkyBlock custom blocks can share vanilla block states, so some item identifications are visual estimates. Hypixel does not endorse this project; client modifications are used at the player's own risk.
